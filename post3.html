Type of crosses in Football.

In football, there are different crosses, which are offered by a wide player (winger player or fullback) to their teammates in penalty box. In this article, we will talk about variety of crosses used in football. There are five different types of crosses in football you can use when looking to cross the ball. They are Low driven cross, High driven cross, Pull back cross, Inswinging cross and Outswinging cross.

The Low driven Cross

The low-driven cross in football is where the ball is played across the goal on the ground, between the penalty spot and the 6-yard box, which is the best place to try and play the low-driven cross (in the middle of the opposition goalkeeper and defenders). This leads to for the opposition team to defend hardly against this type of cross. It causes confusion and incertitude between the goalkeeper and defenders. The high-driven cross

The high-driven cross in football is where a cross is played from a deeper position, toward the back post in the air. The player making the cross will use the laces to generate the power. The purpose of the high-driven cross is to cross the ball to a player who is attacking the back post. This cross can be hard to defend as the opposition defenders have to track the ball as well as watch the player that is running in from behind. When playing the high-driven cross you need to make sure you are using enough power and height so it clears the first man, but not so much that you over-hit the cross. A fullback would more commonly use this type of cross in football.

The pull-back cross

What is pull-back cross? It is a type of crosses when a wide player runs with a ball in parallel with the goal line and then passes back the ball to a player in deep position in order to make an opportunity to score a goal. Vini Junior is known with his pull-back crosses.

The In-swinging cross in football

The in-swinging cross in football is where the wide player will cut inside and use the inside or outside of their foot to play cross into the box. The technique of the kick will make the cross curve towards the goal. An in-swinging cross will usually be played by a team who plays with inverted wingers or fullbacks. This can be a hard cross to defend because the ball will always be moving away from the defender.

The Out-swinging cross in football

An out-swinging cross is the opposite of an in-swinging cross. Instead of cutting inside the winger will go on the outside of the defender and cross using the inside or outside of their foot. The technique used for this type of cross will make the ball curve away from the goal. This is the common type of cross in a football game, as most teams will play with traditional wingers.
